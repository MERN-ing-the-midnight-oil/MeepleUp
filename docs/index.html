Hello Cascade Con 2026 Gamers!!!

My name is Rhys (pronounced "Reese") and I'm a school bus driver here in Bellingham. I've attended every year of Cascade Con so far. Three years ago I completed a coding bootcamp through the University of Washington, and I've been leveling up my app-building skills ever since.

Please consider supporting my journey by beta testing my magnum opus: MeepleUp. MeepleUp is a mobile app that combines the best of event management tools like Eventbrite or Meetup.com and communication tools like Discord, and adds in some special game collection browsing and sharing and also some game ranking magic that can't be found elsewhere. Basically, you can easily browse the combined game collections of your game night friends and discuss asynchronously what to play next.
