Hello Fellow Board Gamers!

My name is Rhys (pronounced "Reese"). I'm a local school bus driver here in Bellingham and a big fan of board games.

Three years ago, I graduated from a UW coding bootcamp, and I've been spending my spare time building an app designed to solve a frequent game-night headache: choosing what game to play.

I'm looking for beta testers for MeepleUp. It's a mobile app that handles your event planning and group chat, but with some extra "magic". You can actually snap a photo of your game shelf, and the app will automatically identify and catalog your games (If you already have your collection on BoardGameGeek, the app will automatically pull your collection from there).

From there, your group can asynchronously browse each other's collections and vote on what to play before the game night even starts. No more spending the first forty-five minutes trying to decide what to play!

I'd love for you to help me test it out and share your feedback. Your input will help shape the future of MeepleUp and make it better for all board game enthusiasts!

Sincerely, Rhys Smoker
